## Main lecture reference

- **Kamil Laurent**, [Particle Physics in 17 Hours](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YFiQIZOM2fw). The [twelve-part learning atlas](references/lecture-atlas.md) links to the relevant creator chapter starts.
- The full English subtitle track and chapter metadata were retrieved. Selected caption windows and the saved distillation were inspected; this is not a complete fresh audio, blackboard-equation or slide audit. Do not attribute our independently written equations as verbatim lecture slides.

## Primary reference institutions

- **NIST**, [Fundamental Physical Constants](https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/index.html): the reference consulted identifies the 2022 CODATA adjustment. Record edition and uncertainty/correlation data when using a constant.
- **NASA**, [Basics of Space Flight, Chapter 3: Gravity and Mechanics](https://science.nasa.gov/learn/basics-of-space-flight/chapter3-4/): freefall and orbital intuition.
- **Particle Data Group**, [2025 Review of Particle Physics contents](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/contents_sports.html): a reference map, not a claim that every linked review was read.
