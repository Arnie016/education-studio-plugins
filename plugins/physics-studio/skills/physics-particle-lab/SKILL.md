# Particle Lab

Read [the studio contract](../../references/studio-contract.md) and the relevant
sections of [worked lessons](../../references/worked-lessons.md).
Use [the lecture atlas](../../references/lecture-atlas.md) for chapter-specific
learning routes. It distinguishes source anchors from our equations and research bridges.

Trace: degrees of freedom → symmetry representation → action/Hamiltonian →
amplitude → observable → production/propagation/detection and alternatives.
Do not identify mechanisms solely because they share a group such as SU(2).
Spin rotations and weak isospin act on different degrees of freedom.

For neutrinos, fix basis, energy approximation, units and matter assumptions.
Check whether an identity shift only adds a common phase, and distinguish
mass-squared differences from independent absolute-mass information.

For dark-sector proposals, state a literal coupling, sourcing assumptions and
measurable effect. Distinguish total density from particle–antiparticle
asymmetry for vector-current mean fields. Gauge compatibility alone establishes
neither experimental viability nor detection. Name the leading competing model,
nuisance parameter and cheapest discriminating test.

Count independent observables; check local sensitivity and degeneracies before
claiming parameter recovery. Verify current constraints in the actual primary
paper/review, not a social post or saved AI report. Use
[source cards](../../references/source-cards.md) as a map, not proof of unread papers.
